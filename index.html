About

A full-scope construction firm building custom homes, commercial spaces, and everything in between across the Greater Toronto Area.

For five years, KCW has treated every project as if it were our own — the conversation that starts on the lot is the same one that finishes at the walk-through. No middlemen. No rotating subcontractors. No corners cut to hit a number.

From ground-up custom homes to commercial fit-outs, we deliver end-to-end: design, framing, millwork, and finish — all licensed, all insured, all in-house.
