Construction Experts

KCW is a construction company focused on delivering high-end custom homes and commercial spaces. Every project is approached with a strong emphasis on detail, structure, and execution.

We handle the full process, including planning, design, permits, and approvals. Construction is completed using skilled, trade-specific crews to ensure each phase meets a high standard.

  3. Architectural Sets & Permit Approval

Why Us

We are known for staying on schedule and maintaining a clean, precise finish. From luxury homes and cottages to commercial interiors, clinics, and hotels, KCW delivers work that reflects quality and discipline.

Project Range

All of our projects fall within a range of $20,000 to $100,000+, covering everything from focused commercial fit-outs to full custom home builds across the GTA.
